📖 2-Minute Free Cloud Setup Guide (Step-by-Step):
  1. Step 1: Open Firebase Console (console.firebase.google.com) with your Google account.
  2. Step 2: Click "Add project" → enter name mine-presets → click Continue → Create Project.
  3. Step 3: In the left sidebar, click Build → Realtime Database → click "Create Database". Select any location → Choose "Start in test mode" (Rules: ".read": true, ".write": true).
  4. Step 4: Copy your Database URL at the top (starts with https://...firebaseio.com) → paste it in the box above → click "Connect & Test" → then click "Push All Local Presets to Cloud Now"!
